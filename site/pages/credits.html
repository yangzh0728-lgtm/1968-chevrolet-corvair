The opening reveal

The exterior and mechanical views were rendered from the Corvair Anatomy model using the same camera and lighting. The mechanical image was digitally retouched to remove an extra visible tire. These illustrations are not photographs of the restoration car.

The interactive model

Corvair Anatomy is the existing interactive model project incorporated into this website. It includes 13 functional assemblies and 4,284 selectable model objects. Choose English or Chinese for the website and model together.

The model is a tool for structural learning. Its modeled details, dimensions, and exploded arrangement should not be treated as factory specifications or a verified workshop procedure.
