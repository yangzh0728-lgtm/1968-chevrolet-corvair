The interactive model

Corvair Anatomy is the existing interactive model project incorporated into this website. It includes 13 functional assemblies and 4,284 selectable model objects. Its explanatory content is currently in Chinese.

The model is a tool for structural learning. Its modeled details, dimensions, and exploded arrangement should not be treated as factory specifications or a verified workshop procedure.
