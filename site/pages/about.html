OUR STARTING POINT

The garage
is our classroom.

We’re learning by working on something real. The restoration gives us a reason to understand mechanical systems, use tools thoughtfully, solve problems together, and ask people with experience how they would approach the work.

The car is at the center, but the people matter just as much: owners who remember their first Corvair, mechanics who know where to look, and engineers who can turn a small detail into a bigger lesson.

WHAT DRIVES US
01

Learn by doing.

Turn curiosity into practical knowledge. Make room for questions, mistakes, and thoughtful problem solving.

02

Keep knowledge moving.

Listen to experienced people and help their stories and skills reach the next generation.

03

Tell the whole story.

Share the process, not just the finished result. Document discoveries, decisions, and the lessons between milestones.
